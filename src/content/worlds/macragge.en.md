Macragge is the homeworld of the Ultramarines Chapter and the capital of the Realm of Ultramar. It lies near the eastern edge of the galaxy, in the southern part of Segmentum Ultima. Primarch Roboute Guilliman grew up on this world, which is known for its orderly government and martial tradition. The planet came under heavy siege during the attack of Hive Fleet Behemoth, and the Ultramarines defended it at great cost.
