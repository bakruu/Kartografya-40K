Mars is the heart of the Adeptus Mechanicus and the most important forge world in the galaxy. Factories, reactors and vast temple complexes blanket its red surface from end to end. The planet is led by the Fabricator-General, who is bound to the rulers of Terra by an ancient treaty. Machines and knowledge are held sacred here, and the weapons and ships built on Mars feed the Imperium's war effort. Despite its closeness to Terra, Mars governs itself by its own rules.
