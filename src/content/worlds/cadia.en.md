Cadia was the fortress world guarding the Cadian Gate, the safest passage out of the Eye of Terror. Everyone born there was raised for military service from an early age, and its regiments ranked among the most respected in the Astra Militarum. The planet held against Chaos incursions for centuries. It was finally destroyed during Abaddon's 13th Black Crusade, a loss that came to symbolize the end of an age for the Imperium.
