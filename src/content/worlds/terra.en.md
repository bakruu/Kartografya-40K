Terra is humanity's birthplace and the seat of government of the Imperium. Structures spanning entire continents cover its surface, the greatest of them being the Imperial Palace. Deep inside the palace, the Emperor has sat motionless on the Golden Throne for ten thousand years. The Astronomican radiates from Terra as a psychic beacon that lets ships find their way through the warp. The Imperium's largest decisions are also made here, by the council of the High Lords of Terra.
