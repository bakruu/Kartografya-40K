Baal is the homeworld of the Blood Angels Chapter. In this system of scorched desert and radiation, the Primarch Sanguinius grew up on the moon Baal Secundus. The Chapter's warriors are still chosen from these harsh lands today. The system came close to annihilation when Hive Fleet Leviathan attacked it during the Devastation of Baal. After the Great Rift opened, Baal was left on the Imperium Nihilus side.
